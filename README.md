### About

Most AI is rented: one model for everyone, trained on someone else's data.
At **Cledion** we build the other kind. Our AI learns from the people who do the work, runs on your own servers, and gets better with every correction.

As CTO I lead engineering across the platform, from model training and evaluation to the desktop and web apps our customers use every day.

### What I'm building

- **OYI (Own Your Intelligence)**: Cledion's platform for training and running models a company owns outright
- **Cledion Stratum**: desktop app that brings that intelligence onto your own machines
- **Cledion for Data Centers**: AI tooling for data-center operations and procurement
- **Monitoring**: keeping models measurable, provable and improving in production
